## 模型介绍

NJU-Earth 融合了**多尺度天气特征预编码技术**与**交叉注意力特征融合技术**。模型通过学习不同空间尺度上的天气特征，并在特征融合阶段建立多类气象变量之间的联系，实现对全球天气演变过程的智能推演。

在全球 0.25° 高分辨率预报模型的基础上，团队进一步构建了系列化 AI 预报体系，将研究范围从全球天气预报拓展到集合预报、区域精细化预报、强降水预测、台风研判以及行星大气模拟等任务。

## NJU-Earth 模型体系

- **BaguanHR**：面向全球中高分辨率集合预报。
- **RainCast**：面向区域强降水预测。
- **RegionalCast**：面向区域精细化天气预报。
- **BaguanCyclone**：面向台风路径与强度研判。
- **NJU-Mars**：面向火星大气环境模拟。
- **CBC-Diffusion**：用于增强降水预报，进一步提升强降水概率预测能力。

## 参与 FDP

参加 FDP 为 NJU-Earth 提供了一个集中展示和检验 AI 气象预报能力的平台。通过统一平台上的预报展示与应用检验，可以更直观地观察模型在不同天气过程中的表现，也有助于推动 AI 预报技术面向真实气象业务持续改进。

NJU-Earth 将以系列化模型体系连接全球预报与专业气象任务，为气象科研、天气预报和防灾减灾提供智能化技术支撑。

**NJU-Earth (Nanjing University)** is now participating in **FDP**. Jointly developed by the School of Atmospheric Sciences at Nanjing University and Alibaba DAMO Academy, NJU-Earth is a global AI weather forecasting model built on the **Baguan** architecture. It produces medium-range and short-range forecasts worldwide at 0.25° resolution.

## Model Overview

NJU-Earth combines **multiscale weather feature pre-encoding** with **cross-attention feature fusion**. It learns weather patterns at different spatial scales and models relationships among meteorological variables during feature fusion, enabling data-driven simulation of global weather evolution.

Building on its global 0.25° forecasting model, the team has developed a family of specialized AI forecasting systems. The research now spans global ensemble forecasting, regional high-resolution prediction, heavy-rainfall forecasting, tropical cyclone analysis, and planetary atmosphere simulation.

## The NJU-Earth Model Family

- **BaguanHR**: global medium- and high-resolution ensemble forecasting.
- **RainCast**: regional heavy-rainfall prediction.
- **RegionalCast**: fine-grained regional weather forecasting.
- **BaguanCyclone**: tropical cyclone track and intensity assessment.
- **NJU-Mars**: simulation of the Martian atmospheric environment.
- **CBC-Diffusion**: precipitation enhancement for improved probabilistic forecasting of heavy rainfall.

## Participation in FDP

FDP provides NJU-Earth with a platform for presenting and evaluating AI-based weather forecasts. Forecast visualization and application-oriented evaluation on a shared platform make it easier to examine model behavior across weather events and support continued improvement toward operational meteorological use.

Through its family of forecasting models, NJU-Earth connects global prediction with specialized meteorological applications and provides intelligent technical support for atmospheric research, weather forecasting, and disaster risk reduction.
